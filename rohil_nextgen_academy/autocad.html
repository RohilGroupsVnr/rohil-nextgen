Choose Classic (3 months) if you are a beginner, Professional (4 months) if you know basic drawing and want 3D and architectural skills, and Expert (5 months) if you want advanced, job-ready training with a live project.

Yes. The Classic program starts from basic drawing concepts. Basic computer knowledge is enough.
